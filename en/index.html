What these numbers tell you — and what they do not

We composite the listed ink samples over the surface at the supplied alpha values, then calculate their sRGB luminance contrast. You see both the minimum and maximum of that sample set. A strong detail never certifies the whole image. The color logo and CityChat contain inks beyond these samples; anti-aliased edges and the final layered composition are not measured here.

Gradient entries show one stop as a solid color. Check every stop and the final position. The overlay.glass sample is approximate and depends on what sits behind it. A larger motif can be easier to notice, but size does not increase its contrast ratio. Use alt="" for decoration, and check meaningful components and text alternatives separately.

Five small moves

Not quite there? Take the next small step.

Let the surface, the spacing and the file choice do the work, while the original artwork stays intact.

  1. L1

    Check the real output

    Inspect strong and soft details on the final surface and at the final size. Test every stop of a gradient.

  2. L2

    Try another surface

    Choose a supplied surface that fits the context and measure again. Avoid adding a plate or inventing a color to mask the issue.

  3. L3

    Choose another file

    Try full / quiet, ijji ink / mint, or CityChat light / dark. For logos, swap the rendition while keeping its original colors.

  4. L4

    Give it some room

    Adjust its size, move it out of the text column, or add clear space. Size does not increase the contrast ratio.

  5. L5

    Leave room for the story

    Remove the motif if needed. Text, buttons and real status components should still tell the complete story on their own.

Before you ship

Check truth before polish

Owner approval authorizes the assets. Each artifact must still prove that its context, size, contrast, fallback, and claims are correct in the delivered output.

1 · Boundary

  • Use the correct Landometer, ijji or CityChat layer
  • Do not generalize F&B, retail, municipality, or CityWiki
  • Cross-city/product comparisons use one schema/release or state incompatibility

2 · Meaning

  • A motif never substitutes for identity; animated logos use only their explicitly named family
  • A real state also has visible status text; the motif does not carry meaning
  • Claims retain source, date, and limits; removing the motif never changes the facts

3 · Asset

  • Path, revision, and SHA-256 match the manifest
  • No redraw, recolor, crop, or distortion
  • Use selected assets, never explorations

4 · Access

  • Decorative marks use empty alt / aria-hidden
  • Meaningful previews have text alternatives
  • Pending state includes copy, time, and cancel

5 · Fallback

  • No-JS and reduced motion show the final still
  • Print and email use static files
  • Motion creates no layout shift or blocked action

6 · Output

  • Test the real viewport and export
  • Social conformance is limited to 1080×1080
  • Video and other ratios are labelled owner extensions
