ijji

ijji’s four-unit motifs belong to ijji. Their assets, meanings, and state use must not be generalized into Landometer capability or portfolio truth.

  • Use only the selected graph-b, rings-c, and rotate-b
  • Motion binds to genuine pending work

Cross-layer rule: a motif never crosses products by default. Promoting an ijji motif into the shared layer needs a new owner decision, revision, and asset record—even though every download lives in this library.

Before you ship

Check truth before polish

Owner approval authorizes the assets. Each artifact must still prove that its context, size, contrast, fallback, and claims are correct in the delivered output.

1 · Boundary

  • Use the correct Landometer or ijji layer
  • Do not generalize F&B, retail, municipality, or CityWiki
  • Cross-city/product comparisons use one schema/release or state incompatibility

2 · Meaning

  • A motif is never a logo, data, or evidence
  • A real state also has visible status text; the motif does not carry meaning
  • Claims retain source, date, and limits; removing the motif never changes the facts

3 · Asset

  • Path, revision, and SHA-256 match the manifest
  • No redraw, recolor, crop, or distortion
  • Use selected assets, never explorations

4 · Access

  • Decorative marks use empty alt / aria-hidden
  • Meaningful previews have text alternatives
  • Pending state includes copy, time, and cancel

5 · Fallback

  • No-JS and reduced motion show the final still
  • Print and email use static files
  • Motion creates no layout shift or blocked action

6 · Output

  • Test the real viewport and export
  • Social conformance is limited to 1080×1080
  • Video and other ratios are labelled owner extensions
